04 / Process Walkthrough

Case study — Wagon Tippler
Motor Base Frame.

How a worn, undocumented base frame at Tata Steel went from field measurement to an approved manufacturing drawing.

01

Observe

Studied the part on site to understand its function, shape, and load-bearing features before any measuring began.

02

Specify

Collected specifications and constraints from the Head of Department and senior engineers on-site.

03

Measure & Sketch

Took accurate measurements with proper metrology tools and recorded key dimensions in initial sketches.

04

Verify

Cross-checked against reference drawings and similar parts already in service to catch measurement error.

05

Model & Draft

Built the 3D SolidWorks model and detailed 2D drawing in parallel, reviewed with seniors for correction.

06

Release

Finalized dimensions, tolerances, materials, and notes to company standard, then submitted for approval.
